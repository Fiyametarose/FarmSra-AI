# Integrated pest management for tomato, potato and maize

## What this is [what]
Integrated pest management (IPM) combines observation, prevention, cultural, physical, biological and—when justified—chemical options.

## Signs to check [signs]
Identify the crop and the actual pest or disease first; different causes can produce similar leaf symptoms.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
Observe how many plants are affected, whether the problem is spreading and whether insects or disease signs are present.

## What to do now [action]
Inspect the crop and record the problem before choosing an intervention. Prioritize measures suited to the condition and farming system.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Use appropriate clean planting material, crop hygiene, locally suitable resistant varieties, agronomic care, crop rotation where relevant and conservation of natural enemies.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
Chemical products are not automatically required. When considered, confirm diagnosis, local crop/problem registration and the official label. Avoid unsupported product brands, doses and schedules.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Use actual field conditions and relevant forecasts to support monitoring; risk varies by disease.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
Compare symptom progression and the outcome of any action. Ask an expert when uncertain.
