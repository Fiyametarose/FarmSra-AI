# Maize: healthy-class image result

## What this is [what]
The image was classified in the healthy maize category. This is not a field-wide certificate of plant health.

## Signs to check [signs]
Look for unexpected spots, pests, leaf yellowing, stem problems, stunting and uneven patches across the field.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
A single photographed leaf can look healthy while other plant parts show a disease or nutrient/water problem.

## What to do now [action]
Inspect other plants and record any symptoms not visible in the uploaded image.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Continue appropriate crop nutrition, water management and monitoring. Select locally suitable seed and use IPM when an actual problem is identified.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
Do not apply a pesticide merely because the application returned a healthy result.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Drought, waterlogging and other conditions may affect crop health without producing one of the model’s listed disease classes.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
Repeat photos or request expert assessment if symptoms become visible later.
