# Safe pesticide decision-making

## What this is [what]
Pesticides are not universal remedies; the correct action depends on whether the problem is fungal, bacterial, viral, caused by mites or not a pest at all.

## Signs to check [signs]
Identify the actual crop, problem and application site before considering a product.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
A product registered for another crop or country is not necessarily permitted for the current case.

## What to do now [action]
Check the official national registration database and the specific product label; ask a trained local adviser if uncertain.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Prefer integrated, lower-risk approaches where effective; protect people, beneficial organisms, soil and water.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
Follow label requirements for personal protective equipment, re-entry time, preharvest interval, environmental precautions, storage and disposal. This educational guide does not specify brands, doses, tank mixes or spray intervals.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Weather can influence application suitability, runoff or drift; follow local label restrictions rather than a chatbot prediction.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
Record the intervention and any adverse effects; verify local regulations. Kenya example: PCPB. In other countries consult the corresponding regulator.
