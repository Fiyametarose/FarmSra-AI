# Tomato mosaic virus

## What this is [what]
Tomato mosaic virus (ToMV) is a tobamovirus that can spread mechanically through infected material, handling and contaminated tools.

## Signs to check [signs]
Light and dark green leaf mottling, distortion or uneven leaf shape can appear; symptoms vary with the plant and virus strain.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
Other viruses and nutrient problems also cause mottling; a model image cannot distinguish every cause without field observations or testing.

## What to do now [action]
Limit movement between suspicious and healthy plants, wash hands and sanitize shared tools, and record which plants show mosaic patterns.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Start with clean seed or transplants, use appropriate resistance where available, and use stringent hygiene; confirmed infected material needs careful management.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
There is no fungicide or insecticide that cures plants infected by tomato mosaic virus. Do not propose a whitefly-control spray as a cure for this mechanically transmitted virus.

## Weather and spread [weather]
This virus is chiefly spread by handling and contaminated material rather than by a simple rain or humidity threshold.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
If many plants show similar mosaic symptoms, seek expert testing before making irreversible removal decisions.
