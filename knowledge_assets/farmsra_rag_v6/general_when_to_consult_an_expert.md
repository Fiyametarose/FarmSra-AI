# When to request an agricultural expert

## What this is [what]
A trained agricultural professional can help when the cause cannot be distinguished reliably from photographs and reported symptoms.

## Signs to check [signs]
Warning signs include rapidly enlarging disease, many affected plants, conflicting signs, unexplained wilting, or symptoms on stems, fruit, roots or tubers.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
Several diseases and noninfectious problems can look similar; a high model score does not replace expert diagnosis.

## What to do now [action]
Collect clear photos, symptom onset, crop, case location, recent field conditions and actions already taken. Ask a question about the specific case.

## Management and prevention [manage]
The farmer selects the expert or available-expert option and gives consent before the case is shared. Do not initiate an automatic referral based on severity.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
Seek qualified local verification before acting on an uncertain or potentially hazardous product choice.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Share relevant dated weather context if available, without claiming it proves the diagnosis.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
Track the request, review advice and ongoing symptoms. Farmer consent may be revoked according to the system workflow.
