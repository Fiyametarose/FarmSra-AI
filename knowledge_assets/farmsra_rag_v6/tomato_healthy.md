# Tomato: healthy-class image result

## What this is [what]
A healthy tomato label is the model’s prediction for the uploaded leaf, not proof that all tomato plants are healthy.

## Signs to check [signs]
Look for spots, mottling, yellowing, curling, insects, unusual fruit markings, wilting or poor growth on other parts of plants.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
An apparently healthy leaf can coexist with a root disorder, viral infection, nutrient imbalance or pest symptoms elsewhere.

## What to do now [action]
Inspect several plants and their leaf undersides; photograph any unusual fruit or stem symptoms.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Continue appropriate irrigation, hygiene, nutrition, spacing and field monitoring.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
Avoid unnecessary pesticide application without a specific diagnosed problem.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Rain, dryness and heat may change disease and stress risk but do not prove a diagnosis.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
Return to the case and upload fresh evidence if new symptoms develop.
