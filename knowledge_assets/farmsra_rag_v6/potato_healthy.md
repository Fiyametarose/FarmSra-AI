# Potato: healthy-class image result

## What this is [what]
The uploaded potato leaf resembles the model’s healthy class; roots and tubers were not necessarily assessed.

## Signs to check [signs]
Look for spotted leaves, abnormal growth, wilting, damaged stems and tubers.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
A normal leaf image cannot exclude late blight elsewhere, soil problems or tuber disease.

## What to do now [action]
Inspect several plants, leaf undersides and available tubers; record any unusual symptoms.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Use suitable healthy seed tubers, balanced crop care, field observation and sanitation practices.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
Do not prescribe any disease control product for a healthy-class prediction.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Conditions may favor future plant disease, but weather does not contradict or prove the current image result by itself.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
