# Tomato yellow leaf curl disease

## What this is [what]
Tomato yellow leaf curl disease is associated with viruses transmitted by the Bemisia tabaci whitefly complex.

## Signs to check [signs]
Young tomato leaves may curl upward, yellow between veins and become small; plants can be stunted and fruit production reduced.

## Conditions that may look similar [check]
Curling may also result from environmental or herbicide problems; whiteflies and plant history are useful clues, but confirm a viral cause when possible.

## What to do now [action]
Inspect young growth and the undersides of leaves for whiteflies, and check healthy transplants nearby.

## Management and prevention [manage]
Use healthy transplants, appropriate resistant varieties and integrated whitefly management; prevent whiteflies carrying virus between susceptible plants.

## Treatment considerations [treatment]
No product cures virus-infected plants. Whitefly control may reduce future transmission where appropriate but does not reverse an existing infection.

## Weather and spread [weather]
Whitefly activity and infected plants drive spread; do not invent a rainfall threshold for this virus.

## Monitoring and expert review [monitor]
Follow new growth and affected plants, and consult an expert if a widespread virus problem is suspected.
