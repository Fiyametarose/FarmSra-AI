SOLANACEAE

{% trans "Tomato Pathologies" %}

Solanum lycopersicum

  • Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Concentric rings, lower leaf yellowing. Prevent with drip irrigation.
  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Water-soaked dark lesions, rapid foliar collapse in cool, humid weather.
  • Leaf Mold (Passalora fulva): Olive-green fungal velvety growth on lower leaf surfaces.
  • Yellow Leaf Curl: Begomovirus transmitted by Bemisia tabaci whiteflies. Upward curling and stunting.
POACEAE

{% trans "Maize Pathologies" %}

Zea mays

  • Gray Leaf Spot (Cercospora zeae-maydis): Rectangular parallel lesions delimited by leaf veins.
  • Common Rust (Puccinia sorghi): Golden-brown to cinnamon powdery pustules on both leaf surfaces.
  • Northern Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum): Cigar-shaped elliptical gray-green lesions.
TUBEROUS SOLANACEAE

{% trans "Potato Pathologies" %}

Solanum tuberosum

  • Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Target-spot brown lesions with chlorotic halos, favored by alternating wet/dry cycles.
  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Oomycete causing rapid rot of foliar canopy and tuber blight.
