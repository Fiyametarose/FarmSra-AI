# Septoria Leaf Spot of Tomato

## Symptoms and spread
Numerous small circular spots with dark margins and pale centers begin on lower leaves; tiny dark fruiting bodies may be visible. Splashing water moves spores from infected residue and nearby plants.

## Prevention and cultural control
Remove diseased leaves when plants are dry, remove crop residue, stake or cage plants, provide spacing, water at the soil surface, mulch to reduce soil splash, control tomato-family weeds and volunteers, and rotate crops.

## Biological and chemical considerations
The source does not establish a biological cure. Protective fungicides may be useful in some production systems, but are not automatically required and cannot repair infected tissue. Verify local registration, crop, use, harvest restrictions and label directions with Kenyan or Ethiopian authorities and local extension services.

## Monitoring and expert assessment
Inspect lower foliage regularly after rain or overhead irrigation. Seek confirmation when early lesions cannot be distinguished from bacterial spot or early blight.
