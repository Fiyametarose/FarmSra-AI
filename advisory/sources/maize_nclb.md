# Northern Corn Leaf Blight
Northern corn leaf blight (NCLB) is caused by the fungus Exserohilum turcicum. It is a common leaf disease in corn.

## Symptoms
Long, elliptical, grayish-green or tan lesions on the leaves, typically 1 to 6 inches long.
Lesions first appear on lower leaves and spread upwards. Severe infections can kill leaves entirely.

## Management
Plant resistant corn hybrids if available in your area.
Manage crop residue, as the fungus overwinters in corn debris. Consider tillage practices that help bury residue.
Rotate crops to non-host plants to reduce disease pressure in the next season.
Consult your local agricultural extension office for region-specific cultural practices and guidance.
