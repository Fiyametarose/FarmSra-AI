# Target Spot of Tomato

## Symptoms and spread
Leaf lesions begin as small brown spots and can develop concentric rings and yellow halos; severe disease causes leaf loss and fruit lesions. The fungus favors prolonged leaf wetness and high humidity and survives on infected debris and hosts.

## Prevention and cultural control
Start with healthy transplants, improve spacing and airflow, avoid prolonged leaf wetness, sanitize production areas, remove infected debris and manage alternate hosts. Rotate away from susceptible crops where practical.

## Biological and chemical considerations
Use an integrated program; this source does not support promising a biological cure. Fungicide decisions should be based on confirmed disease, risk and local extension advice. No Kenya or Ethiopia authorization is established here; use only officially registered products and the exact crop/use label.

## Monitoring and expert assessment
Scout lower and dense canopy leaves during humid periods. Ask an expert to distinguish target spot from early blight and bacterial spot before treatment.
