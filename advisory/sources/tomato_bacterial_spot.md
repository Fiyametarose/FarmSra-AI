# Bacterial Spot of Tomato

## Symptoms and spread
Small water-soaked leaf spots become dark and may merge; fruit can develop raised, scabby spots. The bacteria may arrive on infected seed or transplants and spread by splashing water, workers and equipment, especially in warm, wet conditions.

## Prevention and cultural control
Use tested disease-free seed and healthy transplants. Rotate away from tomato and pepper, remove infected debris, control volunteers and weeds, avoid overhead irrigation, and avoid handling wet plants. Clean tools and equipment between plantings.

## Biological and chemical considerations
No biological treatment is established by this source. Copper-based bactericides may help protect uninfected tissue but do not cure infected plants, and resistance can reduce performance. Product authorization for Kenya or Ethiopia is not established here: consult local extension services and use only an officially registered product whose label lists tomato and the intended use.

## Monitoring and expert assessment
Inspect transplants and lower leaves after wet weather. Seek expert confirmation when symptoms spread quickly or resemble fungal leaf spots before selecting any pesticide.
