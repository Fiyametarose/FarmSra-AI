# Tomato Mosaic Virus

## Symptoms and spread
Leaves may show light and dark green mosaic, distortion and reduced growth or fruit quality. Tomato mosaic virus spreads readily through infected seed, plant material, hands, tools and equipment. Infected plants cannot be cured.

## Prevention and cultural control
Use certified healthy seed and transplants, resistant varieties where available, wash hands before handling plants, disinfect tools and greenhouse equipment, avoid tobacco contamination, remove infected plants and control volunteer tomatoes and susceptible weeds.

## Biological and chemical considerations
The cited source does not establish a biological treatment. Fungicides and insecticides do not cure tomato mosaic virus. Do not apply a pesticide solely for this viral diagnosis.

## Monitoring and expert assessment
Separate suspicious plants and minimize handling. Seek expert or laboratory diagnosis because several viruses, herbicide injury and nutrient disorders can cause similar patterns.
