# Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus

## Symptoms and spread
Young leaves become small, crumpled and upward-curled with yellow margins; plants may be stunted and bushy and lose flowers. Whiteflies transmit the virus. Infected plants do not have a curative treatment.

## Prevention and cultural/IPM control
Use resistant varieties and virus- and whitefly-free transplants. Avoid locating new fields beside older infected fields, use fine-mesh row covers where practical, manage weeds and whiteflies, remove isolated infected plants when incidence is low, and destroy crop residue and volunteers after harvest.

## Chemical considerations
Insecticides cannot cure virus-infected plants and are not automatically required. Any whitefly-control product must be locally registered for tomato and the intended use; verify the official label and consult local extension services. This source does not establish authorization in Kenya or Ethiopia.

## Monitoring and expert assessment
Inspect new growth and whitefly populations. Seek laboratory or expert confirmation because nutrient stress and other viruses can resemble these symptoms.
