# Late Blight in Potato
Late blight is caused by Phytophthora infestans. It is highly destructive and can destroy potatoes rapidly.

## Symptoms
Dark, water-soaked lesions on leaves. During high humidity, white mold appears on the underside of leaves.
Infected tubers have a dry, corky rot that is brownish-red under the skin.

## Management
Plant certified disease-free seed potatoes.
Destroy volunteer potatoes and cull piles.
Monitor weather conditions closely, as the disease spreads rapidly in cool, wet weather.
Consult your local agricultural extension office for region-specific cultural practices and guidance.
