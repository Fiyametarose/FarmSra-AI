# Gray Leaf Spot on Maize

## Symptoms and spread
Lesions become narrow, rectangular and gray to tan, limited by leaf veins. The fungus survives in maize residue; spores spread during warm, humid periods with extended leaf wetness.

## Prevention and cultural control
Choose resistant hybrids, rotate with non-host crops, and manage infected residue with locally appropriate tillage or decomposition practices. These measures reduce inoculum but may not eliminate disease.

## Biological and chemical considerations
The source does not substantiate a biological treatment. Foliar fungicides can be effective when economically warranted, mainly for susceptible hybrids under high-risk, humid conditions with disease present. Local authorization is unverified: consult extension services and use only a product officially registered for maize and this use in Kenya or Ethiopia.

## Monitoring and expert assessment
Scout before and around tasseling, especially in continuous maize and humid fields. Seek expert assessment when lesions move toward upper leaves or before making a fungicide decision.
