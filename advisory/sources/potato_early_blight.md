# Early Blight in Potato
Early blight is caused by Alternaria solani in potatoes. It primarily affects older foliage first.

## Symptoms
Small, dark, necrotic spots with concentric rings (target-board appearance) on older leaves.
As disease progresses, leaves may yellow and die. Tuber lesions are dark, sunken, and circular.

## Management
Ensure plants are adequately fertilized, as stressed plants are more susceptible.
Harvest mature tubers carefully to avoid injury.
Rotate crops so potatoes and related crops are not planted in the same area year after year.
Consult your local agricultural extension office for region-specific cultural practices and guidance.
