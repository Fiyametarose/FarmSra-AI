# Common Rust on Corn
Common rust is caused by the fungus Puccinia sorghi. It is favored by cool, moist weather.

## Symptoms
Small, circular to elongate, cinnamon-brown pustules form on both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
As pustules mature, they rupture the leaf epidermis and release powdery spores.

## Management
Plant resistant hybrids if available in your area. This is the most effective management strategy.
Monitor crops early in the season, especially during cool and moist weather.
Consult your local agricultural extension office for region-specific cultural practices and guidance.
